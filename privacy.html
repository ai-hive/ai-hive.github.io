Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

ai-hive is a personal, single-user system. The only person whose Google data it accesses is its owner, who set it up for their own use.

Data accessed

  • Gmail (read-only): headers and text of new messages in the inbox.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): the owner's events.
  • Google Cloud Pub/Sub: notifications that a new message has arrived.

How the data is used

  • Email content is processed by an AI model running on the owner's own home server to decide whether a message needs attention and to write a short summary.
  • Short summaries (not full email content) may be processed by third-party AI model providers chosen by the owner, to answer the owner's own questions.
  • Summaries, tasks and reminders are stored in the owner's private storage and sent only to the owner.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any purpose other than providing these features to the owner. No data from other people's Google accounts is collected.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ai-hive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Message text (headers and up to the first 2,000 characters) is held only in the system's internal queue on the owner's server and is deleted automatically after at most 7 days. Summaries are kept for up to 14 days, tasks until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
